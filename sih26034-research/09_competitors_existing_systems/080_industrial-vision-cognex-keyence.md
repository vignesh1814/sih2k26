## Why this source matters
Analyzes the gap between high-speed factory inspection hardware and the handheld, mobile-first requirements of SIH26034.

## Key extracted points
- Cognex In-Sight and Keyence systems inspect packages at 500+ items/minute under fixed strobed lighting and stationary conveyor geometries.
- They utilize dedicated optical character verification (OCV) targeting pre-known font templates and fixed pixel bounding regions.
- Why they fail SIH26034: Cannot operate on arbitrary, uncalibrated smartphone photos of unknown packaging taken in uncontrolled warehouse illumination.
- SIH26034 value proposition: Hardware-agnostic computer vision using deep learning models that generalize to arbitrary angles and uncalibrated mobile devices.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Industrial vision relies on fixed optical geometry and telecentric lenses, whereas mobile regulatory compliance demands robust zero-shot scene generalization."
