## Why this source matters
LayoutLMv3 is a leading multi-modal foundation model for Document AI. It jointly models text, layout, and image features, making it highly effective for complex document understanding tasks.

## Key extracted points
- Pre-trained weights are restricted to non-commercial use via the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
- Benchmarks indicate state-of-the-art performance with ~92.1% F1 on FUNSD, ~96.6% F1 on CORD, and ~83.4 ANLS on DocVQA.
