## Why this source matters
Florence-2 is a remarkably lightweight and efficient vision-language foundation model. It treats various vision tasks as sequence-to-sequence translation problems, unifying object detection, captioning, and segmentation.

## Key extracted points
- Available in small parameter sizes (0.23B and 0.77B), allowing it to run on consumer hardware with minimal VRAM.
- Achieves a CIDEr score of 135.6–140.0 on zero-shot COCO captioning, outperforming much larger models like Flamingo (80B).
