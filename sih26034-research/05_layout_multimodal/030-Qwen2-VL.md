## Why this source matters
Qwen2-VL is a highly performant vision-language model series capable of understanding complex images, documents, and videos at any resolution, making it a powerful tool for multimodal extraction.

## Key extracted points
- The 72B model achieves state-of-the-art performance competitive with GPT-4o on benchmarks like MathVista, DocVQA, and RealWorldQA.
- The 2B and 7B variants offer an excellent balance of capability and efficiency under an open-source Apache 2.0 license.
