## Why this source matters
Donut (Document Understanding Transformer) is an OCR-free model that directly maps document images to structured outputs, bypassing traditional OCR pipelines and reducing cascading errors.

## Key extracted points
- Highly effective for end-to-end information extraction and document classification.
- Performs competitively on visual document understanding benchmarks like DocVQA and CORD without requiring separate text-detection steps.
