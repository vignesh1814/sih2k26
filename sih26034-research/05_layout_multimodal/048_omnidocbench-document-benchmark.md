## Why this source matters
Standard benchmark evaluating end-to-end VLM accuracy on complex layouts, text parsing, and table extraction.

## Key extracted points
- Tests models across dense documents, distorted receipts, and real-world packaging images.
- Qwen2-VL scored 69.8% and PaddleOCR-VL scored 96.3% on targeted text recognition subsets.
- Identified that multi-column layouts and non-horizontal text orientations account for 58% of VLM transcription errors.
