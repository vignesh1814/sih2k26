## Why this source matters
Standardized benchmark evaluating LLMs and VLMs on real-world scene text, complex packaging typography, and fine-grained visual question answering.

## Key extracted points
- Evaluates models across 5 core dimensions: Text Recognition, Scene Text VQA, Document VQA, Key Information Extraction, and Visual Reasoning.
- Highlights that while VLMs score > 80% on clean documents, performance drops to 52-64% on distorted retail packaging labels.
- Recommends hybrid architectures combining dedicated OCR tokens with vision-language decoders for robust compliance verification.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "OCRBench v2 exposes the performance divergence between synthetic benchmark documents and distorted, noisy real-world product packaging."
