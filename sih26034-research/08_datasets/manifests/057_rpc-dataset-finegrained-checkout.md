## Why this source matters
Provides multi-angle product photography across 200 distinct grocery SKUs, ideal for multi-view label synthesis and rotation testing.

## Key extracted points
- Image count: 83,739 images across 200 distinct retail product SKUs.
- Multi-view structure: Each SKU photographed from 4 orthogonal horizontal angles plus top view under controlled lighting.
- Relevance to SIH26034: Benchmarks multi-image state fusion, evaluating whether declarations spread across front and back panels are correctly aggregated.
