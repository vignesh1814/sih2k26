## Why this source matters
The definitive open benchmark for Indic script text recognition in the wild, covering Hindi/Devanagari and regional scripts commonly found on Indian FMCG goods.

## Key extracted points
- Image count: 6,582 complex natural scene and packaging images with word-level bounding polygons and Unicode transcriptions.
- Covers Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, and English code-switched text.
- Validates OCR performance on Devanagari numerals and Hindi mandatory generic commodity names (Rule 6(1)(b)).
