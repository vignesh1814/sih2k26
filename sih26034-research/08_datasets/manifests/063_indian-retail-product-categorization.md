## Why this source matters
Provides category-to-rule mapping taxonomy, enabling the compliance engine to route products to specific LMPC exemption tables.

## Key extracted points
- Contains 45,000+ Indian retail product metadata records across 18 major FMCG categories.
- Maps products to specific packaging categories (food, cosmetics, electronics, agricultural commodities).
- Used to test automated classification routing: e.g., identifying scheduled drugs to route to NPPA DPCO exemptions (Rule 26(c)).
