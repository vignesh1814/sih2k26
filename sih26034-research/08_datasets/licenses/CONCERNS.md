## Why this source matters
Documents licensing restrictions of public datasets and details synthetic generation strategy to prevent intellectual property infringement.

## Key extracted points
- SKU-110K: Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; strictly non-commercial. Usable for academic hackathon research, but commercial production model weights cannot be trained exclusively on it.
- RPC Dataset: Academic non-commercial license. Redistribution prohibited.
- Bharat Scene Text: Research access permitted under Bhashini terms.
- Synthetic Dataset Strategy: To eliminate copyright and commercial licensing liabilities, generate procedurally rendered synthetic packaging labels using Pillow and Blender scripts with randomized corporate entities and compliant/non-compliant declaration permutations.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Public retail datasets carry non-commercial restrictions, necessitating procedural synthetic data generation for unrestricted production deployment."
