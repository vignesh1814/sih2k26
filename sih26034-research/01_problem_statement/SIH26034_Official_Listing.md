## Why this source matters
This is the official problem statement driving the entire engineering effort. It defines the explicit functional requirements and the regulatory anchor point (LMPC Rules, 2011).

## Key extracted points
- The system must verify the compliance of packaged commodities against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
- The input modalities explicitly include scanning products, images, and labels.
- Implies a need for a mobile or web-accessible software system capable of OCR and rules execution.
