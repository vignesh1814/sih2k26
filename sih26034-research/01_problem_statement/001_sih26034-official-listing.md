## Why this source matters
Defines the exact problem statement SIH26034: 'Software System to check compliance of Packaged Commodities under Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 by scanning products, images and labels'.

## Key extracted points
- Official mandate: Verification of packaged commodities compliance via optical scanning of products, images, and labels.
- Enforcement scope: Detection of mandatory declarations under Rule 6, Rule 7 PDP dimensions, Table I/II font sizing, and Rule 26 statutory exemptions.
- Deployment goals: Mobile and web-accessible solution for state/central legal metrology enforcement officers and consumer empowerment.
- Evaluation criteria: Algorithmic precision, handling of real-world packaging (curved, reflective, crumpled), speed, explainability, and legal auditability.
