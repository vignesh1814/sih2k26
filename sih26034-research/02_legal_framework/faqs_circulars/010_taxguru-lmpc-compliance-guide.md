## Why this source matters
Provides corporate compliance practice details, seizure procedures under Section 15 of LM Act 2009, and compounding protocols.

## Key extracted points
- Outlines registration under Rule 27 for manufacturers and packers with the Director or Controller of Legal Metrology.
- Explains dual MRP prohibitions and legal precedents where multi-tier pricing in malls vs local stores was held non-compliant under Rule 18(2).
- Details compounding procedures under Section 48 and fine escalations for repeat corporate offenses.
