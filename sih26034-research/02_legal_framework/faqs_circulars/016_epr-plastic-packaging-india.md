## Why this source matters
Covers regulatory overlap between LMPC package labelling and Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) plastic packaging markings.

## Key extracted points
- Category I: Rigid plastic packaging; Category II: Flexible plastic packaging (single or multi-layer); Category III: Multi-layered plastic packaging; Category IV: Compostable plastic.
- Labelling requirements: Mandatory marking of CPCB EPR registration number and plastic resin recycling identification code on packaging.
- Jurisdictional separation: The automated LMPC compliance engine must treat EPR markings as non-LMPC elements, preventing false rejections.
