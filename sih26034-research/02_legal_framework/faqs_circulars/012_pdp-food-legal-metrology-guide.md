## Why this source matters
Breaks down the mathematical and spatial calculation of Principal Display Panel (PDP) surface areas for rectangular cartons, cylindrical cans, and irregular bottles.

## Key extracted points
- Rectangular package: PDP area = Height x Width of the front panel (100% of front face).
- Cylindrical container: PDP area = 40% of (Height x Circumference) of the container.
- Other shapes: PDP area = 40% of the total surface area, excluding top, bottom, and unprintable neck/flange zones.
- Font size mapping: Direct relationship between computed PDP square centimeters and minimum numeral height (Table I).

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "In the case of a cylindrical or nearly cylindrical package, forty percent of the product of height and circumference shall be the principal display panel."
