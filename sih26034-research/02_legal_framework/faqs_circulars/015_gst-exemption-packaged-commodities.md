## Why this source matters
Explains how the definition of 'pre-packaged and labelled commodity' under LMPC was directly incorporated into GST taxation rules, making LMPC compliance critical for tax liability.

## Key extracted points
- Effective July 18, 2022, GST is levied on food items if they satisfy the LMPC definition of 'pre-packaged and labelled'.
- Under Section 2(l) of LM Act 2009, goods packed in advance without purchaser present are pre-packaged.
- If an item is packaged > 25 kg (or > 50 kg for agricultural produce), it is exempt from Chapter II of LMPC, which alters GST tax classification.
- AI relevance: Package weight thresholds (>25kg / >50kg) dictate both LMPC labeling exemption (Rule 26) and tax status.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Pre-packaged and labelled means a pre-packaged commodity as defined in clause (l) of section 2 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009."
