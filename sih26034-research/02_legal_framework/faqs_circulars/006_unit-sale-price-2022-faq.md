## Why this source matters
Establishes the exact mathematical formula, rounding rules, and standard denominators for Unit Sale Price (USP) introduced under G.S.R. 779(E) and 226(E).

## Key extracted points
- Mandatory effective date: October 1, 2022 across all pre-packaged retail commodities.
- Calculation formula: USP = Total MRP (inclusive of taxes) / Total Net Quantity.
- Standard units: Commodities < 1 kg must declare per gram; >= 1 kg declare per kg. Commodities < 1 L declare per ml; >= 1 L declare per L. Length < 1 m per cm; >= 1 m per m. Items by number per N or per unit.
- Rounding rule: Calculated to the nearest two decimal places (half-up rounding).
- Exemption: Packages containing exactly 1 standard unit (e.g., exactly 1 kg or 1 liter) are exempt from separate USP declaration.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Unit sale price shall be declared on every retail package where net quantity is more than one kilogram or one litre or one metre."
