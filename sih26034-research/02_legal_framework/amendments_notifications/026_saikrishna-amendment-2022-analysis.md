## Why this source matters
Provides legal interpretation of Unit Sale Price (USP), electronic products QR code labeling, and Second Schedule omissions.

## Key extracted points
- Clarified enforcement timeline: Transition period granted until October 1, 2022 to exhaust existing packaging inventory.
- Electronic products: Allowed to declare manufacturer details, consumer care, and MRP via a scannable QR code if packaging size is constrained.
- Eliminated Schedule II standard pack sizes, allowing FMCG manufacturers freedom to sell arbitrary net quantities (e.g. 175g biscuits).

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "The omission of the Second Schedule grants manufacturers flexibility in package sizing, subject to strict Unit Sale Price declarations."
