## Why this source matters
Under Section 5 of the Workspace Protocol, an authoritative compliance engine must verify whether any amendments or corrigenda have been notified between 2024 and 2026 before declaring the legal framework baseline consolidated.

## Regulatory Findings & Current Legal Status (as of September 2026)
1. **Consolidated Baseline**: The substantive declaration architecture of Rule 6 and Rule 7 remains anchored by the **2021/2022 amendments (G.S.R. 779(E) & G.S.R. 226(E))**, with full enforcement active from October 1, 2022.
2. **2024 Enactments**:
   - The provisions of the 2023 Amendment Rules (G.S.R. 720(E)) governing electronic spare parts and QR code relaxations took full legal effect on **January 1, 2024**.
   - Circulars issued during 2024 reiterated strict penal action under Section 36 for non-declaration of Unit Sale Price and non-standard symbols like `gms`.
3. **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 Integration**:
   - Decriminalized certain minor weights and measures procedural offences, replacing imprisonment with monetary compounding penalties under the parent Act, but preserved strict statutory penalties under Section 36 for packaging non-compliance.
4. **Conclusion**: No subsequent notifications have superseded Rule 6(11) (Unit Sale Price), Rule 7 Table I (Font height by PDP Area), or Rule 26 exemptions. The rules encoded in this workspace represent the consolidated, active legal standard in India.
