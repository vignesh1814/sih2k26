## Why this source matters
Notified via G.S.R. 779(E) on November 2, 2021, this amendment marked the transition to modern unit pricing, omitted the restrictive Second Schedule on standard pack sizes, and shifted date declarations from "packing date" to "manufacture date".

## Key extracted points
- **Gazette Notification**: G.S.R. 779(E) dated November 2, 2021.
- **Rule 6(11) Added — Mandatory Unit Sale Price**: Mandated declaration of Unit Sale Price on all pre-packaged commodities to facilitate price comparison.
- **Rule 6(1)(d) Modified — Date of Manufacture**: Removed option to declare date of pre-packing or import; mandated strict declaration of **Month and Year of Manufacture**.
- **Omission of Second Schedule**: Abolished the requirement for packaged commodities to be sold only in prescribed standard package sizes (Schedule II), granting packagers design flexibility.
- **Effective Date Adjustments**: Initially slated for April 1, 2022; later deferred via G.S.R. 226(E) to October 1, 2022.
