## Why this source matters
Details the 2023 amendment exempting loose garment sales and electronic spare parts from rigid Chapter II retail labeling declarations.

## Key extracted points
- G.S.R. 720(E): Loose garments sold with inspection rights are exempted from full PDP declarations if size and MRP are provided on hangtag.
- Spare parts sold exclusively for servicing are exempted from standard retail pack size schedules.
- Rule logic tag: Modifies Rule 26 exemption evaluation in the automated compliance engine.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Declarations on loose garments may be made on a hangtag or label attached to the garment, rather than a sealed package label."
