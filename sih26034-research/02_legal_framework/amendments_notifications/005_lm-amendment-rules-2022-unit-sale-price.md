## Why this source matters
Notified via G.S.R. 226(E) (March 28, 2022) and G.S.R. 577(E) (July 14, 2022), these amendments formalized the definitive effective date for Unit Sale Price enforcement (October 1, 2022) and introduced electronic declarations (QR codes) for electronic products.

## Key extracted points
- **G.S.R. 226(E) (28 March 2022)**:
  - Formally set effective date of the Unit Sale Price mandate to **October 1, 2022**.
  - Formulated the exact rounding rule: Unit Sale Price must be declared in Indian Rupees rounded off to **two decimal places** (e.g., `Rs. 0.25 per g`).
- **G.S.R. 577(E) (14 July 2022) — QR Code on Electronic Products**:
  - Permitted manufacturers of electronic products to declare certain mandatory declarations through a **QR Code** for a period of one year, provided MRP, net quantity, manufacturer name, and consumer care remain physically on the package.
- **G.S.R. 648(E) (02 September 2022) — Garments Exemption**:
  - Introduced exemption under Rule 26 for loose garments/hosiery where consumers inspect goods prior to purchase.
