## Why this source matters
Comprehensive legal text detailing the 8 mandatory declarations of Rule 6 and the specific phrasing rules for MRP and consumer care.

## Key extracted points
- Rule 6(1)(a): Name and complete address of manufacturer/packer/importer including city, state, and PIN code.
- Rule 6(1)(b): Common or generic name of the commodity inside the package.
- Rule 6(1)(c): Net quantity in terms of standard unit of weight or measure (strict SI units per Rule 13).
- Rule 6(1)(d): Month and year of manufacture or pre-packing.
- Rule 6(1)(e): Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes, with strict prohibition of sticker overwriting.
- Rule 6(2): Consumer care details requiring contact person/designation, address, telephone number, and email.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Every package shall bear thereon or on label securely affixed thereto definite, plain and conspicuous declarations."
