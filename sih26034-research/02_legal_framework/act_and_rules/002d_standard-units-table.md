## Why this source matters
Rule 13 mandates that all net quantity statements strictly utilize the International System of Units (SI). Using non-standard abbreviations (such as "gms" or "ltrs") constitutes an automatic statutory violation under Section 36 of the Legal Metrology Act.

## Standard Permitted SI Units & Symbols

| Quantity Dimension | Net Quantity Range | Standard Permitted Symbol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mass / Weight** | < 1 kilogram | `g` |
| **Mass / Weight** | $\ge 1$ kilogram | `kg` |
| **Volume / Liquid** | < 1 litre | `ml` or `mL` |
| **Volume / Liquid** | $\ge 1$ litre | `l` or `L` |
| **Length** | < 1 metre | `cm` or `mm` |
| **Length** | $\ge 1$ metre | `m` |
| **Area** | Square units | `m^2`, `cm^2`, `sq m`, `sq cm` |
| **Number / Count** | Discrete items | `N`, `U`, `number`, `unit`, `piece`, `pair`, `set` |

---

## Prohibited Non-Standard Abbreviations (Automatic Violations)

| Prohibited Symbol | Correct Legal Symbol | Statutory Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `gms`, `gms.`, `gm`, `gm.` | `g` | Pluralizing metric symbols or adding trailing periods is illegal under SI system. |
| `kgs`, `kgs.`, `kilo`, `kilos` | `kg` | Non-standard representation. |
| `ltr`, `ltrs`, `ltr.`, `ltrs.` | `l` or `L` | Prohibited abbreviation. |
| `ml.`, `m.l.` | `ml` or `mL` | Trailing period prohibited. |
| `cc`, `c.c.` | `ml` or `cm^3` | Obsolete cubic centimeter notation. |
| `mts`, `mtrs` | `m` | Non-standard meter abbreviation. |

## Department Advisory on Capitalization (2020)
In 2020, the Department of Consumer Affairs issued an advisory to State Metrology Controllers directing that no coercive enforcement action be initiated purely based on capital vs lower-case typographic choices (e.g., `KG` vs `kg`, `GRAM` vs `g`), provided the numeric value and metric meaning are unambiguous. However, non-metric symbols (`lbs`, `oz`, `inches`) or corrupt plurals (`gms`) remain non-compliant.
