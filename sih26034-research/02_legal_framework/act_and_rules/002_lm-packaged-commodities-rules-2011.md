## Why this source matters
The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (LMPC Rules 2011) constitutes the primary regulatory code governing all consumer packaged goods in India. It defines mandatory label declarations (Rule 6), geometric Principal Display Panel requirements and font heights (Rule 7), declaration manner and contrast (Rule 9), placement (Rule 12), standard units (Rule 13), and statutory exemptions (Rule 26).

## Key extracted points
- **Structure**:
  - *Chapter I*: Preliminary (Definitions of Principal Display Panel, retail package, wholesale package).
  - *Chapter II (Rules 3 to 23)*: Provisions applicable to packages intended for retail sale.
  - *Chapter III (Rules 24 to 25)*: Provisions applicable to wholesale packages.
  - *Chapter IV (Rule 26)*: Statutory exemptions from packaging declarations.
  - *Schedules*:
    - First Schedule: Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) on net quantities.
    - Second Schedule: Commodities to be packed in specified standard quantities (largely phased out by 2021 amendment).
    - Third Schedule: Manner of selection of samples.
- **Rule 6 — Core Declarations**: Mandatory display of manufacturer/packer/importer, country of origin, common generic name, net quantity, month/year of manufacture, retail sale price (MRP), dimensions, consumer care, and unit sale price.
- **Rule 7 — Principal Display Panel (PDP) & Font Dimensions**: Geometric calculation of display panel area and mandatory letter/numeral heights (Table I).
- **Rule 9 — Manner of Declarations**: Plain, conspicuous, prominent, distinct contrast with background, prohibited use of misleading language.
- **Rule 18 — Wholesale and Retail Compliance**: Retailers prohibited from selling above MRP; dual MRP strictly prohibited.
- **Rule 26 — Scope Exemptions**: Packages <= 10g / <= 10ml, restaurant food, bulk agri produce > 50kg, and industrial/institutional packs.
