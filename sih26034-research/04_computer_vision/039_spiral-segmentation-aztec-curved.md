## Why this source matters
Provides the mathematical algorithm to dewarp 2D QR codes and Aztec matrix codes printed on curved bottles and flexible pouches.

## Key extracted points
- Utilizes spiral coordinate transformation radiating from the central finder pattern of the 2D code.
- Corrects radial lens distortion and physical packaging curvature without requiring 3D surface reconstruction.
- Improves decode success of curved QR codes (e.g., LMPC e-commerce QR codes under 2022 amendments) from 54% to 96.5%.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Adaptive spiral segmentation reconstructs distorted matrix cells along the curvature vectors of cylindrical packaging surfaces."
