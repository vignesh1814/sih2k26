## Why this source matters
pyzbar is a lightweight, widely used Python wrapper for the zbar library, specializing in reading 1D barcodes and QR codes from images.

## Key extracted points
- Fast and reliable decoding of standard barcode formats without the overhead of deep learning models.
- While it lacks formal modern deep learning benchmarks, it is the de facto standard for simple, rule-based QR/barcode extraction in Python.
