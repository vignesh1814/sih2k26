## Why this source matters
YOLOv11 (and its predecessor YOLOv8) is an industry standard for real-time computer vision tasks, providing versatile support for object detection, segmentation, and classification.

## Key extracted points
- YOLOv11m achieves higher mean Average Precision (mAP) on COCO using ~22% fewer parameters than YOLOv8m.
- Serves as a unified framework that is highly optimized for deployment across edge devices and GPUs using ONNX and TensorRT.
