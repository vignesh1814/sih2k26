## Why this source matters
RT-DETR is the first real-time end-to-end object detection transformer. It eliminates the need for Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS), offering an excellent trade-off between speed and accuracy.

## Key extracted points
- Outperforms YOLO equivalents in speed-accuracy balance by avoiding NMS bottlenecks.
- Available natively in PaddlePaddle and PyTorch, and supported by the Ultralytics framework for easy benchmarking on COCO datasets.
