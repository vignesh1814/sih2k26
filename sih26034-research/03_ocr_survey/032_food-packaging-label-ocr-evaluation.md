## Why this source matters
Directly studies OCR failure modes on supermarket packaged commodities, specular glare, and crumpled packaging.

## Key extracted points
- Evaluated 1,200 supermarket products across rigid boxes, flexible pouches, glass bottles, and aluminium cans.
- Identified glare as cause of 42% of character recognition dropouts in glossy laminate pouches.
- Thin-Plate Spline (TPS) transformation improved character recognition on crumpled flexible packaging by 14.8%.
- Multi-view capture (stitching 2-3 angles) increased complete declaration retrieval from 68% to 94.2%.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Packaging surfaces exhibit non-planar geometries, non-uniform illumination, and specular reflections that degrade vanilla OCR pipelines."
