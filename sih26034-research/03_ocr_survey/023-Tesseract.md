## Why this source matters
Tesseract is one of the oldest and most mature open-source OCR engines, acting as a baseline for numerous applications. It is highly reliable for clean, printed document text extraction.

## Key extracted points
- Uses traditional LSTM-based neural networks for character recognition.
- Benchmarks are heavily dependent on pre-processing (binarization, deskewing); it struggles with complex layouts compared to modern deep-learning models.
