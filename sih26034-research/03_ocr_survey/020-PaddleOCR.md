## Why this source matters
PaddleOCR is an industry-standard, highly efficient open-source OCR toolkit supporting over 100 languages. It balances speed and accuracy, making it ideal for production-ready document parsing.

## Key extracted points
- Supports a wide array of OCR tasks including text detection, recognition, and document structure analysis.
- The recent PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 (0.9B parameters) achieved 96.3% accuracy on OmniDocBench v1.6, while PP-OCRv6 saw a +4.6% increase in detection accuracy over its predecessor.
