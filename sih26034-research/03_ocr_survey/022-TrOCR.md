## Why this source matters
TrOCR introduces an end-to-end Transformer-based architecture for OCR without the need for complex CNN backbones. It excels particularly at handwritten and printed text recognition.

## Key extracted points
- Leverages pre-trained vision (BEiT) and language (RoBERTa) transformers.
- Achieves state-of-the-art Character Error Rates (CER) on the IAM Handwriting Database and performs strongly on SROIE and scene text datasets (e.g., ICDAR2015, SVT).
