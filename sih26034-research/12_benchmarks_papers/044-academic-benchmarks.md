## Why this source matters
Establishes the formal benchmark thresholds required to evaluate each subsystem prior to hackathon judging.

## Target Metric Table
- **OCR Subsystem**: Character Error Rate (CER) < 5%, Word Error Rate (WER) < 8% on clear packaging; > 88% recognition on curved containers.
- **Detection Subsystem**: Mean Average Precision (mAP@50) > 90% for Principal Display Panel (PDP) localization.
- **Key Information Extraction**: F1 Score > 95% for high-liability numeric declarations (MRP, Mfg Date, Net Quantity).
- **End-to-End Decision**: Automation rate > 85%, Human-in-the-Loop review ~15%, False-Positive violation rate strictly < 1%.
