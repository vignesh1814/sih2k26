## Why this source matters
Confirms the technical and mathematical limits of monocular vision systems for estimating physical document and font dimensions without reference targets.

## Key extracted points
- Discloses methods for mapping 2D camera pixel matrices to physical paper and packaging dimensions.
- Establishes that focal length, sensor pixel pitch, and distance-to-object are mathematically entangled; one cannot be resolved without a known physical reference.
- Validates SIH26034 Dead End #047: Automated Rule 7 font-height verification in millimeters requires an explicit physical reference scale (or HITL verification).

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Determining physical dimensional measurements from digital images requires calibration against known physical fiducial markers or optical reference scales."
