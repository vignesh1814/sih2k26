## Why this source matters
Provides algorithms for detecting 1:1:3:1:1 finder patterns in 2D barcodes across arbitrary orientations, perspective skew, and uneven packaging surfaces.

## Key extracted points
- Detects QR finder patterns using run-length edge profiling invariant to package rotation and lighting variance.
- Bootstraps orientation estimation for rectangular packaging panels based on barcode orientation.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "The 1:1:3:1:1 ratio of QR code position detection patterns provides robust geometric anchoring in arbitrarily oriented natural scene imagery."
