## Why this source matters
Confirms the engineering limitation regarding Rule 7 of the LMPC Rules (absolute millimeter font size thresholds). 

## Key extracted points
- Mathematical consensus states that absolute scale (mm) cannot be inferred from a single monocular 2D image without depth data or a known physical reference object.
- Forces the architecture to adopt a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) AR-assisted approach for physical measurements.
