## Why this source matters
Circulars issued by the Central Director of Legal Metrology provide binding administrative interpretations to state enforcement officers, resolving ambiguities on brand marketing, currency symbols, and typographic capitalization.

## Key Extracted Clarifications

### 1. "Marketed By" / Brand Owner Declarations
- **Circular Reference**: WM-10(5)/2016.
- **Guidance**: Packages may bear declarations such as "Marketed by", "Manufactured for", or brand owner details, **provided** the name and address of the actual physical manufacturer or packer is also clearly disclosed on the label.

### 2. Currency Symbol Usage (₹ vs Rs.)
- **Guidance**: Declarations of MRP may legally use either the official Indian Rupee currency symbol `₹` or the traditional abbreviation `Rs.` or `INR`. All are accepted as legally compliant.

### 3. Advisory on Lowercase vs Uppercase SI Units
- **Circular Reference**: WM-10(31)/2020.
- **Guidance**: Field enforcement officers are instructed not to book cases or issue compounding notices solely because unit symbols are printed in capital letters (e.g., `KG`, `G`, `LITRE`), provided the metric net quantity is clear and unambiguous.

### 4. Adhesive Stickers on Pre-Packaged Commodities
- **Guidance**: Affixing individual adhesive stickers to alter MRP or net quantity declarations is strictly illegal under Rule 6, except where explicitly authorized by a temporary Central Government notification (e.g., during GST rate transitions).
