## Why this source matters
Rule 7 establishes the exact geometric definitions for calculating the Principal Display Panel (PDP) area and dictates the absolute physical millimeter height of all mandatory numerals and letters. This forms the legal basis for why 2D monocular cameras cannot verify compliance without metric reference scaling.

## 1. Geometric Definition of Principal Display Panel (PDP)

### Excluded Surfaces (Mandatory Subtractions)
Before calculating PDP area, the following surfaces must be excluded:
- Top and bottom of package.
- Flanges at top and bottom of metallic cans.
- Neck and shoulders of glass/plastic bottles and jars.

### Area Calculation Formulas by Shape
1. **Rectangular Packages**:
   - Area of the principal display panel is the area of the entire side considered the principal face:
   $$\text{Area}_{PDP} = \text{Height} \times \text{Width}$$

2. **Cylindrical or Nearly Cylindrical Packages**:
   - 40 percent of the product of the height of the package multiplied by the circumference:
   $$\text{Area}_{PDP} = 0.40 \times \text{Height} \times \text{Circumference}$$

3. **Other Shapes / Irregular Packages**:
   - 40 percent of the total surface area of the package:
   $$\text{Area}_{PDP} = 0.40 \times \text{Total Surface Area}$$

4. **Small Packages (<= 10 cubic centimeters)**:
   - May be provided on a card or tape firmly affixed to the package.

## 2. Table I — Minimum Height of Numerals and Letters
*(Consolidated pursuant to G.S.R. 629(E), standardizing height requirements across all declarations based on PDP Area)*

| Area of Principal Display Panel ($A$ in $\text{cm}^2$) | Minimum Height (Normal Printed / Inscribed) | Minimum Height (Blown, Formed or Molded on Container) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$A \le 50$** | **1.0 mm** | **2.0 mm** |
| **$50 < A \le 100$** | **1.5 mm** | **3.0 mm** |
| **$100 < A \le 500$** | **2.5 mm** | **4.0 mm** |
| **$500 < A \le 2500$** | **4.0 mm** | **6.0 mm** |
| **$A > 2500$** | **6.0 mm** | **6.0 mm** |

## 3. Typographical and Spatial Layout Constraints

1. **Character Aspect Ratio (Width Rule)**:
   - The width of any numeral or letter shall **not be less than one-third of its height**:
   $$\text{Width} \ge \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Height}$$
   - *Exceptions*: Numeral `1`, lower-case letter `i`, capital letter `I`, and lower-case letter `l`.

2. **Surrounding Clear Space Rule (Net Quantity Declaration)**:
   - To guarantee unencumbered readability, the net quantity declaration must be surrounded by clear blank space:
     - **Above and Below**: At least equal to the height of the numeral declared.
     - **Left and Right**: At least twice the height of the numeral declared.

3. **Contrast and Conspicuousness**:
   - Numerals of retail price and net quantity must be printed in a colour that contrasts conspicuously with the background (Rule 9).
