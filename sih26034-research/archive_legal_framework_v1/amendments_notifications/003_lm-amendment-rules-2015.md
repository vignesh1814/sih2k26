## Why this source matters
Notified via Gazette Notification G.S.R. 385(E) on May 14, 2015, this amendment enacted Rule 6(2), legally mandating comprehensive consumer grievance contact credentials on every consumer package.

## Key extracted points
- **Gazette Notification**: G.S.R. 385(E) dated May 14, 2015.
- **Sub-Rule Added/Amended**: Substituted Rule 6(2).
- **Specific Legal Changes**: Mandated that every package must bear:
  1. Name of the person or office
  2. Full address
  3. Telephone number
  4. Email address of the grievance contact.
- **Rule Engine Impact**: Rule engine must flag a violation if either email address or telephone number is omitted from the consumer care entity block.
