## Why this source matters
Notified through G.S.R. 640(E) and G.S.R. 720(E) (effective January 1, 2024), these rules provided targeted exemptions for electronic spare parts, refined loose retail item rules, and synchronized packaging guidelines with modern industrial supply chains.

## Key extracted points
- **Electronic Spare Parts Exemption**: Exempts spare parts and accessories meant solely for warranty service or industrial repair from retail packaging declarations, provided they are marked *"For Replacement / Servicing Only"*.
- **E-Commerce Loose Commodities**: Enhanced guidelines requiring e-commerce platforms to declare net quantity and unit price for items sold loose or weighed at delivery.
- **Garment Size Notation**: Formally validated international sizing notation (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `XXL`) alongside standard centimeter measurements on apparel.
