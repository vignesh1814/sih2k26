## Why this source matters
Notified via G.S.R. 629(E) on June 23, 2017, coming into force on January 1, 2018, this was one of the most comprehensive revisions to the LMPC Rules. It integrated e-commerce regulation, strictly prohibited dual MRPs, and standardized font size mandates under Table I based on PDP Area.

## Key extracted points
- **Gazette Notification**: G.S.R. 629(E) dated June 23, 2017 (in force from January 1, 2018).
- **Rule 6(10) Added — E-Commerce**: An e-commerce entity shall ensure that mandatory declarations (except month and year of manufacture) are displayed on the digital and electronic network used for e-commerce transactions.
- **Rule 18(2) Amended — Ban on Dual MRP**: Expressly declared: *"No person shall declare different maximum retail prices on an identical pre-packaged commodity."*
- **Rule 7 & Table I Consolidation**:
  - Replaced the confusing separate tables (Table I for weight/volume and Table II for length/area/count) with a single, consolidated **Table I** where minimum height is determined by Area of Principal Display Panel ($A$ in $\text{cm}^2$).
  - Substantially increased minimum font heights to ensure enhanced consumer legibility.
- **Medical Devices Ingestion**: Medical devices declared as drugs brought under LMPC declaration regime.
