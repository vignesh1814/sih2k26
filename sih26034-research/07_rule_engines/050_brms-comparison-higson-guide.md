## Why this source matters
Surveys modern business rules management systems, comparing decision table maintainability, execution latency, and microservice integration.

## Key extracted points
- Compares Drools, Zen Engine, Higson, Camunda, and JSON-Logic across throughput and memory consumption.
- Concludes that decision-table-first engines with JSON native payloads provide the lowest maintenance overhead for regulatory compliance rules.

## Verbatim excerpts (if any)
> "Modern microservice architectures favor lightweight, stateless decision-table engines over monolithic stateful rule engines."
