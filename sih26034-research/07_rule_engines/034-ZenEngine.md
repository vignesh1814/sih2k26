## Why this source matters
Zen Engine by GoRules is a fast, embeddable business rule engine written in Rust that supports evaluating JDN/DMN (Decision Model and Notation) graphs.

## Key extracted points
- Provides extremely low-latency decision execution, making it suitable for high-performance extraction and routing workflows.
- Permissively licensed and easily integrated into various backend architectures via provided SDKs.
